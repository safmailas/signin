Volunteer Hub privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Volunteer Hub is an app that volunteer coordinators use to run events: volunteer lists, shifts, and badge check-in and check-out. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it, including when you sign in with Google.

Who runs it

Volunteer Hub is run by its volunteer coordinators. For questions about this policy or your information, email saffanali@gmail.com.

Signing in with Google

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google asks you to share your name, email address and profile picture with Volunteer Hub, and gives the app a signed sign-in token. Volunteer Hub:

  • checks the token with Google to confirm it’s genuine and that your email address is verified;
  • uses your email address to find the access a coordinator set up for you;
  • saves your Google account ID (a number Google assigns to your account) on that access entry, so only your Google account can use it, and records when you last signed in;
  • doesn’t keep your name or profile picture from Google. The name shown in the app is the one a coordinator entered.

Volunteer Hub never sees your Google password. It doesn’t ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or anything else in your Google account.

The sign-in page (safmailas.github.io/signin) only passes Google’s token back to the app, in the part of the link after the “#”, which browsers don’t send to any server. The sign-in page stores nothing.

What the hub stores

The hub runs on Google Apps Script and keeps its information in a Google Sheet that belongs to the coordinators. It holds:

  • Staff access: your name and role, the Google email a coordinator added for you, your Google account ID, and when your access was created, when it ends and when it was last used. If you use an access code instead, the hub keeps only a scrambled (hashed) form of the code.
  • Volunteer and event information that coordinators enter or import, for example names, contact details, groups, shifts, badges, and check-in and check-out times, plus a log of changes made in the app.

On your device

After you sign in, your browser keeps:

  • your sign-in, so you don’t have to sign in every time. A Google sign-in lasts up to 30 days, and ends sooner if your access ends or is turned off. If you use an access code, your browser keeps the code;
  • a copy of the hub information you’re allowed to see, so the app opens quickly.

“Sign out” in the app (More → Sign out) removes both from that device.

Who else is involved

We don’t sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside the coordinator team. Two services run the app:

  • Google: the hub, its Sheet and Google sign-in run on Google’s services, and Google’s privacy policy applies to them.
  • GitHub: hosts the app pages and the sign-in page. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic visit information such as IP addresses.

Volunteer Hub’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long it’s kept

Your Google email and Google account ID stay on your access entry until a coordinator removes them or deletes the entry. Volunteer and event records are kept for as long as the coordinators need them to run events.

Your choices

  • Sign out in the app to remove your sign-in and the local copy from that device.
  • Ask a coordinator, or email saffanali@gmail.com, to see, correct or delete your information, or to remove your Google sign-in.
  • Remove Volunteer Hub from your Google Account’s third-party connections at myaccount.google.com/connections. This doesn’t delete what the hub has stored; ask a coordinator for that.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
